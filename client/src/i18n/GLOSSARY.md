# Urdu glossary (use these exact words so the whole app reads the same)

Tone: simple, everyday shop-counter Urdu that an auto-parts trader and his staff use. Short. Polite but not formal.
Prefer common loan words that shopkeepers say every day (انوائس, اسٹاک, کیش, بینک) over bookish Urdu.
Keep English brand/product/model names and units as they are (SAZ, CG150, Pcs stays "عدد" only in UI labels, not in item names).
Numbers stay in Western digits (0-9) everywhere. Currency symbol "Rs" stays "Rs".
